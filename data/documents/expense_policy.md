# AcmeTech Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for reimbursing legitimate business expenses incurred by AcmeTech employees while performing company-related activities.

## 2. General Principles

Business expenses must be:

* Necessary for a legitimate business purpose
* Reasonable in amount
* Properly documented
* Approved according to AcmeTech's approval process

Employees are responsible for ensuring that submitted expenses comply with this policy.

## 3. Eligible Expenses

Examples of reimbursable business expenses include:

* Approved business travel
* Client meetings
* Business meals
* Local transportation
* Professional training
* Conference registration
* Approved software or business tools
* Office supplies required for company work

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## 4. Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for individual expenses above **$25**.

The receipt should clearly show:

* Vendor
* Date
* Amount
* Items or services purchased

If a receipt is unavailable, the employee must provide a written explanation when submitting the expense.

Repeated missing receipts may result in rejection of reimbursement requests.

## 5. Business Meals

Business meals may be reimbursed when they have a legitimate business purpose.

The employee must provide:

* Names of attendees
* Business purpose
* Date and location
* Receipt where required

Meals during approved business travel are subject to the limits defined in the AcmeTech Travel Policy.

## 6. Client Entertainment

Reasonable client entertainment expenses may be reimbursed with manager approval.

Expenses above **$250** require department-head approval.

## 7. Software and Subscriptions

Employees should use company-approved software whenever available.

New software or subscription purchases above **$100 per month** require approval from both:

1. The employee's manager
2. The IT procurement team

Employees must not purchase software that creates security or data-privacy risks for AcmeTech.

## 8. Home Office Expenses

Employees approved for remote or hybrid work may claim eligible home-office expenses according to the AcmeTech Remote Work Policy.

Standard internet or electricity expenses are not reimbursable unless specifically approved as part of an employment arrangement.

## 9. Expense Submission Deadline

Expense claims should be submitted through the AcmeTech expense portal within **10 business days** after the expense is incurred.

For business travel, travel-related expenses should be submitted within **10 business days after the trip ends**.

## 10. Approval Process

Expense reports up to **$500** require approval from the employee's direct manager.

Expense reports above **$500** require approval from:

1. The employee's direct manager
2. The department's designated Finance approver

## 11. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are generally not reimbursable:

* Personal purchases
* Personal entertainment
* Traffic or parking fines
* Luxury upgrades
* Unapproved software
* Expenses incurred by family members
* Personal portions of business trips

## 12. Currency Conversion

Expenses incurred in foreign currencies should be submitted in the original currency.

The expense system will convert the amount using the company's approved exchange rate for the transaction date.

## 13. False or Misleading Claims

Submitting intentionally false or misleading expense claims may result in disciplinary action.

## 14. Exceptions

Exceptions require written approval from Finance and the relevant department head.

## 15. Contact

Questions regarding expense eligibility, reimbursement status, or policy exceptions should be directed to the AcmeTech Finance team.
