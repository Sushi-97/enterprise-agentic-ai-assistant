# AcmeTech Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the leave benefits available to full-time employees of AcmeTech and explains the process for requesting and approving leave.

## 2. Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to **20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year**.

Annual leave is credited at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees who join during the year receive annual leave on a prorated basis.

Employees are encouraged to plan long periods of leave in advance to minimize disruption to their teams.

## 3. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year.

Any unused annual leave above 5 days expires at the end of the year.

Carried-forward leave must be used before **March 31** of the following year.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**.

For sick leave lasting more than **3 consecutive working days**, the employee may be required to provide a medical certificate.

Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

## 5. Casual Leave

Employees receive **6 days of paid casual leave per calendar year**.

Casual leave is intended for short-notice personal requirements.

A maximum of **2 consecutive casual leave days** may normally be taken at one time.

Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward.

## 6. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may receive parental leave according to applicable local employment laws and AcmeTech HR guidelines.

Employees should contact the HR team at least **30 days before the expected start date**, whenever possible.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to **5 paid working days** of bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member.

Immediate family includes:

* Spouse or domestic partner
* Parent
* Child
* Sibling
* Parent-in-law

Additional unpaid leave may be approved by the employee's manager and HR depending on the circumstances.

## 8. Leave Request Process

Employees must submit leave requests through the AcmeTech HR portal.

For planned annual leave:

* Leave of 1–2 working days should normally be requested at least **3 working days in advance**.
* Leave of 3–5 working days should normally be requested at least **1 week in advance**.
* Leave longer than 5 working days should normally be requested at least **2 weeks in advance**.

Emergency or sick leave may be submitted without advance notice.

## 9. Approval

Leave requests are reviewed by the employee's direct manager.

Approval depends on:

* Available leave balance
* Team workload
* Project deadlines
* Existing team leave schedules

Managers should respond to planned leave requests within **2 working days** whenever possible.

## 10. Holidays and Weekends

Public holidays and weekends occurring during an approved leave period are not deducted from the employee's annual leave balance unless local regulations require otherwise.

## 11. Unpaid Leave

Employees who have exhausted their available paid leave may request unpaid leave.

Unpaid leave requires approval from both:

1. The employee's manager
2. Human Resources

Approval is based on business requirements and the reason for the request.

## 12. Leave During Probation

Employees may use accrued sick and casual leave during probation.

Planned annual leave during probation requires manager approval and should generally be limited to **5 consecutive working days** unless an exception is approved by HR.

## 13. Contact

Questions regarding leave balances, eligibility, or exceptions should be directed to the AcmeTech Human Resources team.
