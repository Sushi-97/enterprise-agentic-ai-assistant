# AcmeTech Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for business travel undertaken by AcmeTech employees.

## 2. Travel Approval

All business travel must be approved before booking.

Domestic travel requires approval from the employee's direct manager.

International travel requires approval from:

1. The employee's direct manager
2. The relevant department head

Employees should obtain approval before making any non-refundable booking.

## 3. Booking Process

Flights and hotels should be booked through the AcmeTech corporate travel portal whenever possible.

Employees should generally choose reasonable and cost-effective travel options.

## 4. Air Travel

For domestic flights, employees should book economy class.

For international flights:

* Economy class should normally be used for flights shorter than 6 hours.
* Premium economy may be approved for flights between 6 and 10 hours.
* Business class may be approved for flights longer than 10 hours with department-head approval.

## 5. Hotel Accommodation

Employees may book hotels up to the following limits unless an exception is approved:

* Tier 1 cities: up to $220 per night
* Tier 2 cities: up to $160 per night
* Other locations: up to $120 per night

These limits exclude applicable taxes.

Employees should choose hotels close to the business location whenever practical.

## 6. Meals

Employees may claim reasonable meal expenses during approved business travel.

The standard daily meal limit is **$60 per day**.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless they are part of an approved client or company event.

## 7. Local Transportation

Reasonable expenses for the following may be reimbursed:

* Taxi
* Rideshare
* Public transportation
* Airport transfers
* Rental cars where business needs justify the cost

Luxury vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable.

## 8. Personal Travel

Personal travel may be combined with a business trip if:

* It does not increase the cost to AcmeTech
* The employee obtains manager approval
* Personal expenses are paid by the employee

Additional hotel nights, meals, or transportation related to personal travel are not reimbursable.

## 9. Travel Changes and Cancellations

Employees should notify their manager and the travel team as soon as possible if approved travel plans change.

Where possible, bookings should be cancelled or modified to minimize financial loss to the company.

## 10. Expense Submission

Travel expenses must be submitted through the AcmeTech expense portal within **10 business days** after the trip ends.

Employees must provide receipts for individual expenses above **$25**.

## 11. Travel Advances

Travel advances may be provided for certain international trips.

Advance requests must be submitted at least **7 business days before departure**.

Any unused amount must be returned within **5 business days** after the trip.

## 12. Travel During Leave

Business travel should not normally be scheduled during an employee's approved personal leave.

If an employee voluntarily interrupts approved leave for urgent business travel, the employee should contact HR so that the affected leave days can be reviewed and adjusted if appropriate.

## 13. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the employee's department head and may also require Finance approval depending on the cost.

## 14. Contact

Questions about travel bookings, reimbursements, or travel-policy exceptions should be directed to the AcmeTech Travel and Finance teams.
